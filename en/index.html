You set the pressure

Strong hands work here. You decide how much.

The place

From the street to the curtain.

You step in from the street into a room with a green wall, a rattan lamp and a golden Buddha painting. Behind it, the treatment rooms: aubergine silk curtains tied with tassels and tables dressed in red Thai silk.

For couples there is a room with two tables side by side.
